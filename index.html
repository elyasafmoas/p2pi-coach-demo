How this works

This is a friendly demo for the classroom. Every coach reply is scripted — there's no live AI, no backend, and no accounts. The "thinking…" pause and word-by-word reveal are simulated to make it feel like a real AI coach.

In the real product, the same experience would use:

  • An AI model for natural, plain-language conversation.
  • Trusted lesson content the AI is only allowed to teach from, so answers stay accurate and safe.
  • A safety layer that spots when someone is asking for personal advice and steps in before answering.

Our golden rule: the coach teaches how things work, but never tells you what to do with your money. That choice always stays with you.
