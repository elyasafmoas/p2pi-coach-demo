How this works

This is a friendly classroom demo — no live AI, no backend, no accounts. There are two tabs plus your coins:

  • 📚 Learn — short courses with quizzes that teach the basics.
  • 📈 Simulate — do it: replay real market history with pretend shekels, then ask the built-in Coach about your own result.
  • 🪙 My Coins — tap the coin counter up top to see the rewards you've earned for learning and exploring.

The Coach's replies are scripted, and the "thinking…" pause and word-by-word reveal are simulated to make it feel like a real AI coach. In the real product this would be an AI model grounded in trusted lesson content, with a safety layer that spots advice-seeking before answering.

Our golden rule: the Coach explains how things work and what your result did, but never tells you what to do with your money. That choice always stays with you.
